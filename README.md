Overview
Socioplastics is a long-duration transdisciplinary artistic research and epistemic infrastructure connecting architecture, urbanism, conceptual art, environmental research, visual archives, media, classification and public knowledge. Its fundamental unit is the addressable node. Ten nodes form a chapter, 100 nodes a Book, and ten Books a Tome. Concepts recur across strata while indexes, identifiers and public interfaces make the structure navigable.
Scope distinction: This 11K release is a structural and bibliographic index. The published pages and Century Packs represent the 11K architecture. The machine-readable files already present in this GitHub repository may still cover only nodes 0001–4000. Do not describe the repository as an 11,000-record downloadable dataset until its records have been generated and audited.
A connective reading of the 11K field
Socioplastics is not assembled around a single discipline. It is constructed through addressable relations: a text can be situated by number, revisited by a later text, located inside a Century Pack — https://antolloveras.blogspot.com/p/socioplastics-books.html, and interpreted through the living Project Index — https://antolloveras.blogspot.com/p/socioplastics-project-index.html. Its scale is therefore a spatial problem as much as a quantitative one. NumericalTopology (991) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18991243, ScalarArchitecture (993) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18998246 and MapDimensioning (2505) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19889238 establish positions through which individual works enter a navigable structure.
A marker is an entrance, not a label pasted onto a finished idea. FlowChanneling (501) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18678959 makes directed passage a structural operation; SemanticHardening (503) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18680418 addresses the persistence of vocabulary; TopolexicalSovereignty (508) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18682343 joins naming to spatial and conceptual position. These operators belong to the Operational Decalogue — https://antolloveras.blogspot.com/2026/02/520-socioplastics-510-systemic-lock.html, while RecurrenceMass (994) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18998404 gives a second route into the later structural physics. Read across them rather than as a glossary.
Architecture is not restricted to buildings. The essay Architecture Without Buildings (9503) — https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33339264 can be read alongside Architecture as Load-Bearing Structure (1505) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19162193, StructuralCoherence (2504) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19888714 and the Tome X archive — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/p/socioplastics-tome-x-closure.html. Together these entries open a route from material construction toward the architecture of public knowledge, without claiming that their texts are interchangeable.
Urban experience enters the field as a condition of evidence. Urbanism as Territorial Model (1506) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19162265 and Civic Permeability and Friction Regimes (809) — https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.31563688 can be approached through the CiudadLista reading of 11K — https://ciudadlista.blogspot.com/2026/10/socioplastics-at-11k-urbanism-spatial.html. FieldEnvironment / Tome VI — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/p/socioplastics-tome-vi-fieldenvironment.html and the environmental research reading — https://fieldeco.blogspot.com/2026/10/socioplastics-at-11k-environmental.html provide other angles from which to compare spatial observations with conceptual structure.
The archive is operative rather than merely retrospective. CyborgText (2901) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19913674, MasterIndex (2909) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19920664 and LegibleArchive (2910) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19921092 link text, metadata, retrieval and persistence. The archives and memory essay — https://otracapa.blogspot.com/2026/10/socioplastics-at-11k-archives-memory.html and museology essay — https://freshmuseum.blogspot.com/2026/10/socioplastics-at-11k-museology.html are autonomous readings of these conditions, not promotional copies of the authorial index.
Art and moving images alter the routes through which a field can be read. Conceptual Art as Protocol System (1502) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19161373 connects to the contemporary-art reading — https://artnations.blogspot.com/2026/10/socioplastics-at-11k-contemporary-art.html; Media Theory as Mediation Framework (1507) — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19162359 can be followed into the moving-image reading — https://tomototomoto.blogspot.com/2026/10/socioplastics-at-11k-moving-image.html, the digital-culture reading — https://youtubebreakfast.blogspot.com/2026/10/socioplastics-at-11k-digital-culture.html, and Tome IX — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/p/socioplastics-tome-ix-moving-images.html. The relations are interpretive passages: they do not erase the independence of each channel.
Recursion also has to face its limits. The Institutional Thresholds Core (9011–9020) — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/2026/07/socioplastics-9011-authorityfragment.html and Epistemic Passages Core (9041–9050) — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/2026/07/socioplastics-9043-claimdecay-core-xv.html introduce AuthorityFragment (9011) — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/2026/07/socioplastics-9011-authorityfragment.html, ClaimDecay (9043) — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/2026/07/socioplastics-9043-claimdecay-core-xv.html and VerificationRefusal (9047) — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/2026/07/socioplastics-9047-verificationrefusal.html. Alongside the field-formation essay — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/2026/10/socioplastics-at-11k-field-formation.html, they keep questions of uncertainty, authorization and evidence within the structure itself.
The 11K state is a dated opening, not an endpoint. The post-10K Tome XI — https://socioplastics.blogspot.com/p/socioplastics-tome-xi-post-10k-field.html extends the numbered field, while twelve October disciplinary readings — https://antolloveras.blogspot.com/p/socioplastics-project-index.html offer different interpretive entrances. Specific works can be identified through their DOIs, the Books provide routes into their sequences, and the Project Index documents the relations among them. GitHub is one interface to that connective system; it is not a replacement for the primary texts or their persistent records.
